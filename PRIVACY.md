# Privacy notice

Last updated: 2026-07-27

Investment Research OS is a private, single-operator research prototype.

## Reddit

The Reddit Data API request was rejected on 2026-07-25. The application does
not access Reddit APIs, scrape Reddit, automate Reddit-targeted web search, or
collect, retain, process, redistribute, or send Reddit content to model
providers.

General operator-initiated web search may incidentally display a Reddit result.
That result remains an untrusted discovery lead outside the application. The
application does not ingest or retain its text, snippet, URL, username,
engagement data, or derived signal.

If a lead identifies a filing, approval, trial record, issuer document, or
paper, the operator must locate the original source. Only independently
retrieved, validated primary-source material may enter a research record.

## Active research data

Active integrations collect only fields required by their documented research
contracts. Evidence retains source attribution, cutoff dates, retrieval dates,
and content hashes for audit. Credentials and raw provider secrets must not be
stored in research records, logs, this repository, or issue reports.

Questions can be raised through this repository's issue tracker. Do not include
private or sensitive information in an issue.
